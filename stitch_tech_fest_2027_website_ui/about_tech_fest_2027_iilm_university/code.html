NOT SILOS.
SYNERGIES.

Higher education historically segregated thinkers into isolated corridors: coders in terminal pits, electrical engineers in clean labs, business operators in lecture halls, and artists in design bays.

TECH FEST 2027 operates on a single architectural truth: breakthrough inventions occur precisely where disciplines smash together at high velocity. Our festival is engineered from the ground up to synthesize high-level theoretical computer science with heavy industrial fabrication and venture acceleration.
