CULTURAL FINALE PROTOCOLS & FAQS

Can non-participating external students attend only the Bollywood Night concert?

expand_more
Access to Tech Bollywood Night requires an official TECH FEST 2027 Delegate Pass. General external students can register for free as "General Festival Delegates" prior to the registration close date. Unregistered walk-ins will be denied entry at campus security checkpoints.

Will campus transport shuttles operate after the concert concludes at 23:30?

expand_more
Yes. Dedicated campus transit shuttles will dispatch delegates continuously from the main campus gate along Golf Course Road directly to Sector 53-54 Rapid Metro Station and Sikanderpur Metro Interchange until 00:30.

What security rules and bag policies are enforced at the Amphitheater?

expand_more
Only small clear pouches or festival delegate kits are permitted. Backpacks, outside food and beverages, sharp objects, laser pointers, and aerosol cans are strictly confiscated at the perimeter. Security marshals and medical emergency teams will be active on site.

When will the secret headlining musical artist be officially announced?

expand_more
The full cultural lineup, including our guest Bollywood producer and vocalists, will be officially unveiled exactly 14 days prior to the festival across all official IILM social feeds and registered delegate email dispatches.
