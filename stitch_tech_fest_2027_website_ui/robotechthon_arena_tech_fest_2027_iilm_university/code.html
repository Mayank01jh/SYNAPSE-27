[TECHNICAL SCRUTINEERING & PIT PROTOCOLS]

ARENA SAFETY REGULATIONS

ZERO-TOLERANCE PROTOCOLS. Any breach during technical check-in results in immediate disqualification and pit de-licensing.
PROTOCOL 01 lock

Weapon Locking Pins

High-visibility red mechanical locking pins must immobilize all spinners, pneumatic bars, and flippers anytime the robot is outside the designated combat cage.

MANDATORY IN ALL PITS
PROTOCOL 02 wifi_off

Radio Failsafe

Every transmitter and receiver pair must execute an immediate automatic motor cutoff (≤ 1.5 seconds) upon transmitter signal loss or battery disconnect.

TESTED ON TEST BENCH
PROTOCOL 03 battery_alert

Battery Containment

Only LiPo / LiFePO4 packs up to 6S (25.2V max) permitted. Batteries must be housed in rigid, fireproof shock-isolated armor. Mandatory LiPo fire bags during pit charging.

NO EXPOSED CELLS
PROTOCOL 04 block

Zero Flame / Fluids

Pyrotechnics, open combustion, glue sprays, liquids, conductive confetti, or entanglement nets are categorically banned. Pure physical and rotational kinetic transfer only.

KINETIC ONLY
PROTOCOL 05 masks

Mandatory Crew PPE

All pit engineers and spotters must wear ANSI Z87.1 certified eye protection, Kevlar reinforced impact gloves, and fire-retardant aprons when tuning live weapons.
