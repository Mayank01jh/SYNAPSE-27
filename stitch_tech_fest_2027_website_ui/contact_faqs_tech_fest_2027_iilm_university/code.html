CATEGORIZED FAQ DIRECTORY

UPDATED HOURLY BY SECRETARIAT
SECTION 01

REGISTRATION, ELIGIBILITY & SQUAD PROTOCOLS

[4 DIRECTIVES]
Who is eligible to participate in TECH FEST 2027? expand_more
Undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma students enrolled in any recognized national or international higher education institution are fully eligible. A valid college photo ID card or current semester proof of enrollment must be displayed during physical entry accreditation.
Are festival delegate registration passes completely free of cost (₹0)? expand_more
Yes. General delegate passes, AI track entries, competitive programming submissions, and amphitheater keynote access are ₹0 complimentary courtesy of university endowments and corporate sponsors. Certain specialized hardware arena brackets (such as 15kg/30kg RoboWars) may mandate a nominal refundable safety deposit for pit workbench allocations.
Can solo attendees register and assemble squads on-site? expand_more
Absolutely. We host a dedicated "Squad Assembly Mixer" 90 minutes prior to the 24-hour Hackathon kickoff at Central Amphitheater Mezzanine. Solo hackers, UI designers, and systems architects will be paired using skill-matching telemetry tags.
Can an individual register for multiple events across conflicting tracks? expand_more
You may register for multiple tracks; however, you are strictly responsible for managing your physical availability. Scrutineering calls for RoboWars and Hackathon pitch rounds cannot be rescheduled for overlapping team representatives.
SECTION 02

TRAVEL, ACCOMMODATION & HOSPITALITY

[3 DIRECTIVES]
Is overnight stay permitted on campus for the 24-Hour Hackathon? expand_more
Yes. Accredited 24H Hackathon squads and verified pit crew members are permitted to stay inside designated indoor innovation hubs overnight. Campus security remains deployed 24/7 with continuous badge checks. External non-accredited attendees must vacate campus grounds by 23:00 IST.
Are meals, refreshments, and hydration stations provided? expand_more
Accredited participants receive complimentary subsidized meal tokens for lunch and midnight fueling stations (coffee, energy refreshments, and fresh snacks). Water replenishment stations are positioned at every 50 meters throughout the festival pavilions.
Are sleeping pods or rest zones available for outstation teams? expand_more
Separate male and female resting zones equipped with padded recliners, charging banks, and monitored baggage deposit lockers are established in the Campus Sports Complex. Priority is allocated to outstation squads traveling more than 150 km.
SECTION 03

TECHNICAL HARDWARE, POWER & NETWORK STANDARDS

[3 DIRECTIVES]
What electrical power supply and pit bench facilities are provided? expand_more
Every verified workbench receives a dedicated 230V, 50Hz, 16A/6A surge-protected multi-plug power hub. Regulated DC bench power supplies (0-30V, 5A) and basic digital multimeters are available at the Central Pit Tool Crib upon university ID collateral.
Can teams bring personal soldering equipment and LiPo battery charging stations? expand_more
Personal temperature-controlled soldering irons and certified smart LiPo chargers are permitted ONLY inside the ventilated Arena Pit Workshop Zone. Open flame torches and charging bare LiPo batteries without an explosion-proof safety pouch are strictly forbidden.
What are the campus Wi-Fi specifications and developer access parameters? expand_more
Dual-band Wi-Fi 6 enterprise infrastructure covers all festival zones under SSID TECHFEST-BUILDERS-5G. High-throughput gigabit Ethernet lines are installed at every hackathon sprint table. Ports 22 (SSH), 80, 443, and common cloud registries (Docker, HuggingFace, GitHub) are unrestricted.
SECTION 04

BOLLYWOOD CONCERT NIGHT CLIMAX PROTOCOLS

[2 DIRECTIVES]
Do registered event participants require a separate pass for the Bollywood Concert? expand_more
All actively competing delegates holding an RFID-tagged wristband have automated, guaranteed entry to the Central Amphitheater Concert Enclosure. Non-participating student visitors must claim a separate free "Cultural Night Wristband" from the ticket booth on Day 02 before 16:00 IST.
What are the security inspection protocols and prohibited amphitheater items? expand_more
Mandatory metal detector screening is enforced at the Amphitheater perimeter. Prohibited items include outside food/liquids, glass bottles, laser pointers, oversized backpacks, professional recording tripods without media credentials, and unverified electronic assemblies.
SECTION 05

PRIZES, INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & CERTIFICATION

[2 DIRECTIVES]
Who retains ownership of the intellectual property (IP) created during the festival? expand_more
You retain 100% full intellectual property rights to all code, prototypes, mechanical assemblies, and venture materials created during TECH FEST 2027. Neither IILM University nor participating corporate sponsors claim equity or rights over participant inventions.
When are cash rewards distributed and certificates authenticated? expand_more
Trophies and commemorative mock checks are awarded during the Grand Valedictory Stage Ceremony on Day 02. Monetary prize grants are disbursed via direct bank wire transfers within 14 banking days following tax compliance verification. Digitally verifiable blockchain certificates are issued via email within 72 hours.
