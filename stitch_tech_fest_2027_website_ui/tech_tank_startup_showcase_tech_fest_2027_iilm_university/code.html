BATTLEFIELD RULES

Tech Tank maintains uncompromising standards to protect founder integrity and provide genuine venture exposure. Review the four founding operational protocols below:

PROTOCOL 01 // FUNCTIONAL MVP MANDATORY

Pure concept slide decks without code will be screened out in Stage 1. Squads must demonstrate a live working software sandbox, physical hardware prototype, or validated deployment telemetry.

PROTOCOL 02 // ZERO EQUITY COMMISSION

Neither IILM University nor the TECH FEST 2027 Committee takes any equity, warrants, or intellectual property rights. All prize grants are non-dilutive, and equity agreements with angels are founder-sovereign.

PROTOCOL 03 // STRICT CHRONOMETER CUTOFF

A hard hardware buzzer cuts founder microphone audio at 180 seconds sharp (3:00). Squads immediately transition into the 5-minute investor defense and Q&A session.

PROTOCOL 04 // TERM SHEET DISBURSEMENT PROTOCOL

Prize grants disburse upon milestone verification through the IILM E-Cell incubation committee. Angel syndicates execute bilateral standard SAFE or convertible note agreements following festival completion.

[VENTURE CLARIFICATIONS // DUE DILIGENCE FAQ]

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Direct, unambiguous answers regarding team composition, equity governance, IP protection, and external participation.

CAN SOLO FOUNDERS APPLY WITHOUT A SQUAD? expand_more
Yes. Solo founders are fully eligible to apply and pitch, provided they have built a working prototype or MVP themselves and can handle both the technical and business defense portions during the 5-minute investor cross-examination.
DO WE NEED AN INCORPORATED PRIVATE LIMITED ENTITY? expand_more
No formal legal incorporation (Pvt Ltd, LLP, or C-Corp) is required during the festival. Pre-incorporation student projects, research prototypes, and beta apps are fully eligible. If awarded seed investments from participating angel syndicates, the IILM E-Cell can assist your squad with zero-cost incorporation protocols post-festival.
ARE NON-IILM EXTERNAL UNIVERSITY SQUADS ELIGIBLE? expand_more
Yes. Tech Tank is an open pan-India intercollegiate arena. We invite verified student founders from all accredited institutions across India. External finalists receive official campus guest accreditation, transit passes, and stage rehearsal access.
WHO OWNS INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND SOURCE CODE? expand_more
100% of all intellectual property, patent rights, repositories, and trademark assets remain exclusively with the student founders. Neither IILM University nor TECH FEST 2027 exercises any claim, right-of-first-refusal, or equity encumbrance over participating startups.
